I’m a software engineer in San Francisco. My work has taken me through data science consulting, biotech, government technology, and applied AI.

Most recently, I worked at Inkeep, helping enterprise customers use AI in their support experiences. I enjoyed working closely with customers and seeing how those relationships shaped the product.

Before that, I worked at U.S. Digital Response, helping governments adopt technology they could use and sustain independently, and at Reverie Labs, building software for AI-driven drug discovery. Applying AI to better medicines is still something I’m excited about.

Earlier, I was a data scientist at Miner & Kasch, a data science consulting firm, building machine learning for Fortune 500 and startup clients and teaching multi-day PySpark and machine learning courses.

Things I’m curious about:

  • Agent orchestration: running many agents on a job too big for one
  • Fine-tuning small models: the applications where they can replace a frontier model
  • The AI stack: everything from power plants and chips up to the apps on your phone
  • Agentic engineering: what software developers become when agents write most of the code
  • Vertical AI: putting AI to work in medicine, government, and other places where the stakes are real
  • AI slop: how people keep up when AI makes more of everything
  • AI that writes like me: getting a model to sound like a person, not a press release

Some things I’ve written and taught.
